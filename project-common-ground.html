Common Ground

An identity system for a community-led cultural programme, built to feel civic without becoming institutional.

Role

Discipline
Brand / Campaign
Year

Common Ground / Identity Framework

01 / Brief

A shared identity without a single voice.

Common Ground brought together independent organisers, artists, and local spaces. The identity needed enough consistency to be recognisable while still leaving room for each participant to sound like themselves.

02 / Direction

Structure as the common language.

Rather than relying on a fixed logo as the main device, the system uses type, line work, spacing, and a repeatable information structure. The result can move from a small programme card to a large poster without losing its character.

Campaign / Modular Composition

03 / Result

Recognisable, flexible, and human.

The final system feels coherent because its rules are clear, not because every application looks identical. That balance allows the identity to belong to the community rather than sitting above it.
